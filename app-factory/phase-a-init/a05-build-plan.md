## Step entry

### Phase A — Init

#### Step 5: Entry point `update-main-app`

**Must achieve:** the entry for an update to the main application.

**App worked on:** App 1.

**Joins the pipeline:** A change to what the app does takes the feature path from Step 12; any other update goes to the step David names.

**Health check first:** Yes, before anything else. The procedure is [[[pdlf:pdlf-library/operating-procedures/sop-health-check|SOP: Health Check]]](C:/pdlf/pdlf-library/operating-procedures/sop-health-check.md).

**The orchestrator asks David:** Which application; whether the update changes what the app does; the update statement and its short name; for an update that is not a feature, the step to start from.

**Entry record:** established and written to the ledger as section 4 of [[[pdlf:app-factory/pdlf-build-plan|PDLF Build Plan]]](C:/pdlf/app-factory/pdlf-build-plan.md) sets out.

**Carried out by:** the orchestrator (Step 1).
