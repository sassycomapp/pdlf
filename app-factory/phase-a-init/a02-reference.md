# Step 2 — New application, no context: Reference

What the orchestrator does at Step 2, in order. Agent-facing. Fixed values: [[[pdlf:app-factory/phase-a-init/a02-config|Step 2 Configuration]]](C:/pdlf/app-factory/phase-a-init/a02-config.md). It is carried out at item 8 of [[[pdlf:app-factory/phase-a-init/a01-reference|Step 1 Reference]]](C:/pdlf/app-factory/phase-a-init/a01-reference.md), after the choice of entry; that reference then completes the entry: the slug checks, the business row, the entry row, the receipt and the stop.

1. Ask David for the business's name and the mode, `full` or `test`. Explain that `test` lets the steps that allow it be abbreviated, and that `full` runs every step in full.
2. Check the business folder holds only `{slug}-app1`, and `{slug}-app1` holds only `.git`. If it does not: stop and raise a deviation, because a business that already holds material is entry 03.
3. Return to item 9 of [[[pdlf:app-factory/phase-a-init/a01-reference|Step 1 Reference]]](C:/pdlf/app-factory/phase-a-init/a01-reference.md).
